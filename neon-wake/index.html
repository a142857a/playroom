Controls

Weapons fire automatically.

Steer
Drag on the playfield, or use WASD / arrow keys.
Forms
Tap a form or press 1–3. Switching pauses fire for 0.45 seconds; you can still steer.
Focus
Tap Focus or hold Shift to steer slowly.
Pulse
Tap Pulse or press X to clear bullets and damage enemies.
Pause
Tap Pause, or press Space or P. Use the same control to resume. Outside immersion, Esc also pauses or resumes.
Other actions
Retry or R restarts the sector. Sound or M toggles audio. Wipe all progress opens a confirmation.
Display
Top-right symbols switch language, open this Guide, enter fullscreen, and enter immersion. Exit or Esc leaves immersion. On short screens, Tab from the playfield opens immersion to keep keyboard controls visible.

Three forms

Spread: bullets spread evenly across a fan. Move closer to land more hits.

Missiles: each missile picks a target and finds another if it is destroyed.

Laser: a continuous beam that follows reachable enemies ahead. It switches away from destroyed or shielded targets. Higher ranks improve damage and tracking.

Pickups

Rank +1Improves every weapon, shield and pulse.

Pulse ammoAdds 1 pulse. Carry up to 3.

RepairRestores 50 HP, up to 100. Rare; only drops while hurt.

InvincibilityNo damage for 12 seconds.

Triple damageDeal 3× damage for 15 seconds.

HomingFor 18 seconds, Spread bullets seek enemies and Laser curves through up to eight enemies ahead.

Bosses

Meteors: fall in staggered showers without a warning. Fly between the rocks.

Phase shield: bosses ignore damage for 3 seconds between phases. Keep dodging.

Touch targets: touch both green rings, then attack the exposed core for 6 seconds. Watch for nearby bullets.

Rotating lasers: watch the warning lines, then follow the beams or stay inside the central circle. Up to 12 beams; other guns pause.

Walls: fly through each opening. Later bosses send up to five walls with narrower gaps.

Sweeping laser: moves sideways after a warning. Dodge through the marked gap.

Homing fireballs: purple fireballs keep chasing you. Orange fireballs turn briefly, then fly straight. Avoid the solid head; the flame trail cannot hurt you.

Keep your progress

Starting ranks are 1, 6, 11, and so on. Continue to the next sector to keep a higher earned rank. Replays and retries use the fixed starting rank. Failed attempts lose their upgrades. Remaining pulses carry into the next sector. New runs, replays and retries start with 1 pulse.

Stay alive

Only the light-blue core takes hits. Shields automatically block bullets and fireballs. Lasers, walls, meteors, and all enemy collisions bypass shields.

Pulses clear bullets and deal equal damage to enemies and bosses, but cannot pierce boss shields.
